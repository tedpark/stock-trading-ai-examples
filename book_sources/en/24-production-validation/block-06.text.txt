Symptom: a strategy working fine through Tuesday flips direction on Wednesday's opening bar
Cause:   on Monday night, yfinance retroactively updated adjusted prices →
         the three months of closing prices stored in DuckDB all shifted slightly →
         the HMM retrained with different parameters → the regime label order flipped
Fix:     compare cached values with new values on the same date →
         if they differ by more than 1%, invalidate the cache + rebuild the affected window
