1. Data pipeline
   Record the market as numbers.

2. Feature engineering
   Turn raw data into questions the model can read.

3. HMM
   Read the current market regime as probabilities.

4. Pairs / Z-score
   Measure how far two stocks have spread apart.

5. Regime Z-score
   Judge whether that spread matters under the current regime.

6. SAC RL
   Read all of the above and choose the position size.

7. Risk / Broker
   Send only actions that pass the risk limits as real orders.
